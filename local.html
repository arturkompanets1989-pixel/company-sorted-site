What we do · Starting a business on the island

The company is one door out of five.

We will show you the other four and the order they open in: tax registration, your basis to work in your own company, the permits for your premises, and the council’s trade licence. Then we keep the dates for you every year.

Who this door does not fit: a shop, where a foreign investment under $5 million closes retail completely; a school, where a non-resident may hold no more than 40% without Board of Investment approval. A café, restaurant or studio is on none of the restricted lists, so it can be 100% yours; whether a café with a shop counter counts as retail, our partner firm answers before you pay.

    The roles the law requires from day one

    A company secretary, whose consent goes in with the application. The director can be you alone: the law does not require a director to live here.

When you organise it yourself

A company, but no basis to work in it

You register a company and are left with the question of what lets you work in it. Nobody usually asks it before taking the money for the registration.

How Sorted runs it

We ask it first and say what is required

A business visa is a visit visa: it allows no employment, paid or unpaid. The published routes are three: the immigration investor category, from $100,000 with at least 10% of the voting shares; the tourism route through the tourism authority, LKR 20 million per investor or director, at most two per company; and the Board of Investment, for projects it has approved. We set your plans against these and tell you the result, including “none of them fits”. We do not arrange visas.

Five questions to settle before you pay

Who does the work and signs?
Company Sorted runs your case and the service. The registration, the secretarial work and the signatures come from a Sri Lankan partner firm registered to do it. We do not call ourselves accountants and we do not sign your filings.
Who do I pay?
World Sorted (Private) Limited, against a written invoice that lists the work. You pay the partner firm nothing. Registrar’s fees and office fees are separate lines, at cost.
What do I have to do in person?
For the registration, nothing at the offices: you bring paper documents to our partner firm’s office. In person you are needed at the bank, to open the company account. If your place registers with the tourism authority, it wants paper copies certified by an attorney-at-law or a justice of the peace, delivered by courier or by hand, and it inspects the premises. And the food premises application goes in with a medical report on the staff who handle food.
Do you arrange my visa?
No. We tell you what the published routes require and whether your plans meet them, before you pay for the company. The visa itself is your application to the offices that issue it.
