We register the company and map the other four doors

You live here, so there is no power of attorney and no couriering of originals: you bring paper documents to our partner firm's office yourself.

1

Company name and registration

We check your options and take the name through approval. The application, the articles and the consents go in together. You get the certificate of incorporation.

2

Tax registration within 30 days

Every company must register with Inland Revenue within 30 days of incorporation (IRD notice SEC/PN/IT/2026/02). You get the confirmation.

3

The roles the law requires

A company secretary, and an authorised person who must be an individual living in Sri Lanka (Companies Act, s. 130C(1)). The director can be you alone.

4

The other four doors, in order

Which permits your place needs, in what order, and by when, counted from the day you plan to open.

What to know before you start

  • This door does not fit a shop: a foreign investment under $5 million closes retail completely.
  • A school: a non-resident may hold no more than 40% without Board of Investment approval.
  • A business visa is a visit visa and allows no employment. We set your plans against the published routes and tell you the result, including “none of them fits”.
  • Permit fees are not in the $490: each office and council sets its own, and we do not name a figure until our partner firm confirms it.

Price: $490 for the registration. Proposed price: confirmed in your written quote before you pay. Registrar's fees are about $55 with one director, at cost and without our margin; “about”, because the Registrar may also charge for the name declaration.

Common questions

Before you register

Who does the work and signs?

Company Sorted runs your case and the service. The registration, the secretarial work and the signatures come from a Sri Lankan firm registered to do it. We are not accountants and we do not sign your filings.

Who do I pay?

World Sorted (Private) Limited, against a written invoice that lists the work. Registrar's fees and office fees are separate lines, at cost.

What do I have to do in person?

For the registration, nothing at the offices: you bring paper documents to our partner firm's office. In person you are needed at the bank, to open the company account.

Do you arrange my visa?

No. We tell you what the published routes require and whether your plans meet them, before you pay for the company. The visa itself is your application to the offices that issue it.
