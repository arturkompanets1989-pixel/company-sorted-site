Who does the registration and the filings?

Company Sorted runs your case and the service. The specialist work and the signatures come from a Sri Lankan firm registered to do it. We are not accountants and we do not sign your filings.

Who do I pay?

World Sorted (Private) Limited, against a written invoice that describes the package. Government fees are a separate line, at cost, without our margin.

Can I open the company remotely?

Most stages can be prepared while you are at home. Not the personal step at the bank, and not the authorised person, who by law has to live in Sri Lanka.
