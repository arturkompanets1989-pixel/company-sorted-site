Questions to settle before you pay

Who does the registration and the filings, and who signs?
Company Sorted runs your case and the service. The specialist work and the signatures come from a Sri Lankan partner firm registered to do it. We are not accountants and we do not sign your filings.
Who do I pay?
World Sorted (Private) Limited, against a written invoice that describes the package. You pay the partner firm nothing. Government fees are a separate line, at cost, without our margin.
Can I open the company remotely?
Most stages can be run while you are at home: the forms, the signatures, the tax registration. Everything except the personal step at the bank and the authorised person, who by law has to live in Sri Lanka. The bank and some procedures may also ask for you in person or for extra documents; we find that out before you pay.
What do I have to come here for in person?
The bank account. A company account is opened in person: the published rules hold no remote route. The bank asks for a copy of a valid visa or permit and a utility bill no older than three months, so we do not promise the account on one short trip. We tell you what your bank asks for before you pay.
Who is the authorised person?
Every company has to appoint a person living in Sri Lanka who keeps its register of beneficial owners (Companies Act, s. 130C(1)). You cannot hold this role from abroad. Who holds it for your company is settled with the partner firm, in writing, before you pay.
Which documents do I need?
A certified copy of your passport, proof of your home address, your signatures on the registration forms, the company name and what it will do, and the details of any other shareholder or director. Sri Lanka does not accept an apostille, so your documents are certified another way; which way, for your country, we tell you in writing before you pay.
Why trust a new company?
We do not publish invented reviews. Instead: a company you can look up, World Sorted (Private) Limited, PV 00376250; a written contract; a stated list of work; and a document for every stage done.
