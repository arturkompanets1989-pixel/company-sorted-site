What to know before you start

  • The board appoints and removes the secretary; the previous one does not have to agree, unless your articles say otherwise. We check the articles first.
  • The previous firm may delay handing over the records, and the law sets no duty or deadline for that. So we do not promise a complete set in advance.
  • If the company is not linked to the director's eROC login, a separate request to the Registrar is needed. The Registrar does not publish how long it takes, so we do not name a date.
  • Timing depends on your documents, the previous firm's reply and government procedures.

Common questions

Before you change firms

Do I need my old accountant's permission?

Changing your accountant and changing your registered company secretary are not the same thing. The board changes the secretary unless your articles say otherwise, so we check the articles first. Handing over the books held by the previous firm can need a separate arrangement.

What if the previous accountant will not hand over the documents?

We list what is missing, ask for it in writing and work out what can be rebuilt from other sources. We do not promise to obtain what a third party will not release.

My company missed filings. Is that inside the $990?

No. First we look at what was missed and what it needs. Past years are priced separately. Nobody can name the final late-filing figure in advance: the Registrar decides it. After that, the company can move to regular care.

Can I switch if I am outside Sri Lanka?

Most of the handover: the board resolution, the forms and the letters to the Registrar. Some steps or certifications can require you in person or extra documents; we find that out before you pay.
