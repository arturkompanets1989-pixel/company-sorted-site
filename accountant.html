What the $190 handover is made of

Changing firms is not one form. The previous secretary often holds the registered office, your records and the beneficial ownership role as well, so it takes five or six filings. All of them are in.

  1. 01

    Where your company stands now

    What your articles say about changing the secretary, who is listed as secretary and director, where the registered office is, who holds the beneficial ownership role. You get it in writing before anything is filed.

    Included

  2. 02

    Board resolution, Form 20 and Form 19

    The board removes the previous secretary and appoints the new one; Form 20 is filed with the new secretary’s consent on Form 19, within 20 working days of the change.

    Included

  3. 03

    Registered office, Form 13

    If your office is registered at the previous firm’s address, it moves. The change takes effect at least 5 working days after the Registrar receives the notice.

    Included if the address changes

  4. 04

    Where your records are kept, Form 14

    Notice of the new place for the company’s records and registers, within ten working days.

    Included if the place changes

  5. 05

    Beneficial ownership, forms BO 5 and BO 6

    The authorised person for beneficial ownership and the place where that register is kept. Every company must appoint an authorised person: an individual living in Sri Lanka (Companies Act, s. 130C(1)). A person from our partner firm takes the role.

    Included

  6. 06

    Access to your company’s eROC account

    Access is requested from the Registrar for you as director, not for the previous firm. We write the request; you see it the day it goes and the answer the day it comes.

    Included

  7. 07

    Your records from the previous firm

    A written request and the list of what has to be kept at the registered office: eleven kinds of records, including ten years of financial statements. We handle the correspondence.

    Included
