Comparing the ways

You can do this without us. Here is the comparison.

Opening a company with one director, as the example.

Do it yourself: about $55

Government fees only. By far the cheapest route, and you come out understanding your own company. You still need a registered office, a secretary and an authorised person living in Sri Lanka, and every date is yours to remember.

A local secretary: $202 to register

eSecretary, with government fees; then $89 a year for a foreign owner. Much cheaper than us. They know the offices and publish their prices. Bookkeeping and the tax return are not in that price, and keeping several firms in step is your job.

Through us: $1,700 first year

Registration, office, secretary, bank documents, bookkeeping and the first filings, in one package, in English, and the dates are our job. We cost more than both. We are new: no clients to show yet. We do not sign your filings; the partner firm does.
