What the $2,490 is made of

Not just two registrations. Eight pieces of work, without which the structure does not reach the land registry or get through its first six months.

  1. 01

    Two companies

    The company that holds the land and the company that uses it, both registered through our partner firm. You get the registration documents of both.

    Included

  2. 02

    The shares of the land-holding company

    51% held by a local partner, 49% by you, and you are the director with signing rights. The second company is wholly yours. The law counts foreign shareholding direct and indirect, and it has to stay below 50%. You get the founding documents and the register of shareholders.

    Included

  3. 03

    Transfer of the land to the land-holding company

    The notarial deed of transfer and its registration at the land registry it is presented to. You get the registered deed.

    Included

  4. 04

    Proof of the shareholding at registration

    When the deed is registered, the Registrar of Lands asks the company secretary for documents showing the share. We prepare them and follow the filing.

    Included

  5. 05

    A lease to your own company

    A lease of up to 99 years, the longest the law allows, from the land-holding company to the company that is wholly yours. Signed before a notary and registered at the same land registry. You get the registered lease.

    Included

  6. 06

    Filing for registration on time

    A deed signed before a notary goes to the Registrar within 30 days of the notary’s attestation; the duty sits with the notary. We follow it and send you the proof of registration.

    Included

  7. 07

    The first six-monthly letter to the Registrar of Lands

    The six months count from the day the deed of transfer is registered. The law asks for the letter in writing from the company secretary and sets no official form; it is written to the text of the law, and we have a sample. You get a copy of the letter sent.

    Included

  8. 08

    Beneficial ownership forms for both companies

    For each company, the form on its beneficial owners and the form on its authorised person. You get both sets as filed.

    Included
