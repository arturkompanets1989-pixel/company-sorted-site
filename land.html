What to know before you start

  • If the foreign share reaches 50%, the transfer is void from that date, and the law gives six months to bring it back below half (Land (Restrictions on Alienation) Act No. 38 of 2014, ss. 2(2), 4(4)).
  • The lease held by your own company cannot be mortgaged to a bank licensed under the Banking Act for five years from the date it is signed. Do not plan a local loan against it in those years.
  • The authorised person of each company must be an individual living in Sri Lanka (s. 130C(1)). Who holds that role is agreed in writing before you pay.
  • We found no published fee for registering the lease, so the partner firm names it before work starts.
