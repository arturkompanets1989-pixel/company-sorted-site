What to know before you start

  • Nobody can name the final figure: the Registrar sets the sum for late filing at his own discretion, and we cannot lower it.
  • The fine for a late annual return is up to LKR 100,000 for the company and up to LKR 50,000 for each officer in default, and only on conviction by a court (Companies Act, s. 131(4)).
  • Each past year needs audited statements. The Registrar has published no procedure for auditing several years at once, so the audit is scoped and priced before you start.
  • The Registrar does not publish how long linking the account takes, so we do not name a date.

Price: Named in your written quote, from the number of open years. Government fees are a separate line, at cost: the annual return fee is LKR 9,322 with VAT, $27.78, for each year.

Common questions

Before you catch up

Can you lower what the Registrar charges?

No, and we say it first. The Registrar sets the sum for late filing at his own discretion. What we change is that you know what the bill is made of before you pay any of it.

Who does the work and signs?

Company Sorted runs your case and the service. The accounting and secretarial work, and the signatures, come from a Sri Lankan firm registered to do it. The annual return is signed by a director and by the secretary.

Who do I pay?

World Sorted (Private) Limited, against a written invoice that lists the work. Government fees and any sum the Registrar sets are separate lines, at cost.
